# FD Calculator: Estimate Fixed Deposit Maturity and Interest

A **Fixed Deposit (FD) Calculator** estimates how much a deposit may be worth at maturity based on the principal, interest rate, tenure, and compounding method. It helps compare hypothetical outcomes before choosing a bank or deposit product.

## What Is a Fixed Deposit?

A fixed deposit is a financial product in which money is placed with a bank or other eligible institution for a chosen period under specified terms. The interest rate, payout method, minimum deposit, premature withdrawal rules, and applicable protections depend on the institution and product.

## Compound Interest Estimate

For a deposit where interest compounds at a fixed rate and no money is added or withdrawn, a common formula is:

**A = P × (1 + r/n)^(n × t)**

Where:
- **A** = estimated maturity value
- **P** = initial deposit
- **r** = annual interest rate as a decimal
- **n** = compounding periods per year
- **t** = tenure in years

For example, an illustrative ₹1,00,000 deposit at 7% per year compounded quarterly for 2 years would have an estimated maturity value of about ₹1,14,888 before any applicable taxes or product-specific adjustments. Actual bank calculations may differ based on day-count conventions, payout options, and terms.

## How to Use an FD Calculator

1. Enter the principal deposit.
2. Enter the offered annual interest rate.
3. Select the tenure.
4. Choose the compounding or payout option if available.
5. Calculate the estimated maturity value and interest.
6. Confirm the result against the bank's official quote and terms.

## Cumulative vs. Regular Interest Payout

With a cumulative deposit, interest is generally reinvested according to the product terms and paid with the principal at maturity. With a non-cumulative option, interest may be paid periodically. The maturity value and cash-flow pattern differ, so choose the calculator mode that matches the actual product.

## Important Things to Check

- Whether the rate is fixed for the full tenure.
- The compounding frequency and payout method.
- Premature closure rules and any reduced interest or penalty.
- Tax treatment and whether tax may be deducted.
- Deposit eligibility and any applicable deposit-insurance limits.
- Special rates or conditions for particular customer categories.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the calculator result guaranteed?

No. It is an estimate based on entered assumptions. Confirm the maturity amount with the institution before opening the deposit.

### Is FD interest taxable?

Tax treatment depends on applicable law and the depositor's circumstances. Check current official guidance or consult a qualified tax professional.

### Can I withdraw an FD early?

Rules vary by product. Premature withdrawal may reduce interest or incur a penalty, and some deposits may have restrictions.

## Final Thoughts

An FD Calculator can help you compare deposit amounts and tenures, but the advertised rate is only one part of the decision. Review the full terms and verify the expected payout directly with the institution.
