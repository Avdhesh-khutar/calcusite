# Salary Hike Calculator: Calculate Increment Percentage and New Salary

A **Salary Hike Calculator** helps estimate a raise amount and the resulting salary after an increment.

## Salary Hike Formula

**Hike Amount = Current Salary × Hike Percentage ÷ 100**

**New Salary = Current Salary + Hike Amount**

For example, a 10% hike on a monthly salary of ₹40,000 is ₹4,000, making the new monthly gross salary ₹44,000 before deductions.

## How to Use It

1. Enter your current salary.
2. Enter the proposed hike percentage.
3. Calculate the increment and new salary.
4. Confirm whether you are comparing monthly or annual figures.

## Gross Salary vs Take-Home Pay

A hike in gross salary does not always translate to the same percentage increase in take-home pay. Taxes, provident fund contributions, insurance, allowances, bonuses, and other deductions can change the net amount.

## Compare Offers Carefully

Look beyond the headline percentage. Compare fixed pay, variable pay, benefits, work location, working hours, growth opportunities, and the effective date of the raise. For annual comparisons, use the same definition of salary for both offers.

## Reverse Calculation

If you know the old and new salary, percentage hike is:

**(New Salary − Old Salary) ÷ Old Salary × 100**

Use consistent periods and salary components for a meaningful comparison. Calculator results are estimates and do not replace an official compensation letter.
