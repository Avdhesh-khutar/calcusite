# Ideal Weight Calculator: Understand Healthy Weight Estimates

An **Ideal Weight Calculator** provides a rough reference range based on measurements such as height and sometimes age or sex. Different formulas can produce different estimates, so the result should not be treated as a personal medical target.

## Why Estimates Differ

Common ideal-weight formulas were developed for specific purposes and populations. They may not account for muscle mass, body composition, pregnancy, age-related changes, ethnicity, or individual medical history.

## How to Use the Calculator

1. Enter the measurements requested by the tool.
2. Calculate the estimate.
3. Read it as a broad reference, not a diagnosis.
4. Consider the result alongside other indicators and professional advice.

## Weight and Health

Body weight alone cannot describe overall health. Blood pressure, blood tests, physical fitness, sleep, nutrition, medical history, and how a person feels may all matter. A person with a high muscle mass may weigh more without having the same health profile as someone with less muscle.

## Use the Number Carefully

Avoid crash diets or drastic changes based only on an online result. If weight management is a goal, sustainable habits and individualized guidance are generally more useful than pursuing a single calculated number.

## Disclaimer

This calculator is for general information only and does not diagnose a condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for advice tailored to your health, especially if you are under 18, pregnant, recovering from illness, or managing a medical condition.
