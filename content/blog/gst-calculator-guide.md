# GST Calculator: Calculate Tax, Inclusive Price, and Base Amount

A **GST Calculator** helps estimate Goods and Services Tax on a price. It can calculate tax added to a base amount or separate the tax component from a price that already includes GST.

The applicable GST rate depends on the product or service classification and current rules. Confirm the correct rate before using a result for invoicing or compliance.

## Calculate GST on a Base Price

When GST is added to a price before tax:

**GST Amount = Base Price × GST Rate ÷ 100**

**Price Including GST = Base Price + GST Amount**

Example: If a taxable base price is ₹1,000 and the assumed GST rate is 18%:

- Base price: ₹1,000
- GST amount: ₹180
- Total including GST: **₹1,180**

This example illustrates the arithmetic; it does not imply that 18% applies to every item.

## Extract GST From an Inclusive Price

If the total price already includes GST:

**Base Price = Inclusive Price × 100 ÷ (100 + GST Rate)**

**GST Amount = Inclusive Price − Base Price**

For a total of ₹1,180 at an assumed 18% rate, the base price is ₹1,000 and the GST component is ₹180.

## CGST, SGST, and IGST Basics

In many applicable domestic transactions within a state, GST may be split into Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST). Inter-state transactions may involve Integrated GST (IGST). The correct treatment depends on the transaction and applicable tax rules.

## How to Use a GST Calculator

1. Enter the base price or GST-inclusive price.
2. Select whether GST should be added or extracted.
3. Enter the applicable rate.
4. Calculate the tax component and total.
5. Verify the classification, rate, and tax treatment for the transaction.

## Common Mistakes

- Applying a rate without checking the product or service classification.
- Adding GST to a price that already includes it.
- Using the wrong inclusive-price formula.
- Ignoring rounding or invoice-specific requirements.
- Assuming every transaction uses the same CGST/SGST or IGST treatment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is GST always 18%?

No. Rates vary by goods and services and can change. Check current official tax guidance for the specific item or service.

### How do I remove GST from a total?

Use the inclusive-price formula: base price equals total multiplied by 100 and divided by 100 plus the GST rate.

### Can I use a calculator for official tax filing?

A calculator can help with arithmetic but does not determine legal classification or compliance. Verify amounts and requirements using official guidance or a qualified tax professional.

## Final Thoughts

A GST Calculator is useful for quick estimates and checking price breakdowns. For invoices, accounting, or tax filing, confirm the correct rate and treatment using current official rules.
