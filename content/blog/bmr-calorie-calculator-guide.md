# BMR and Calorie Calculator: Estimate Basal Metabolic Rate

A **BMR and Calorie Calculator** estimates basal metabolic rate (BMR)—the energy the body uses for basic functions at rest—and may estimate daily energy needs after considering activity.

## BMR vs Daily Calorie Needs

BMR is not the same as total daily energy expenditure. Daily needs also include movement, exercise, and the energy used to digest food. A calculator may multiply BMR by an activity factor to estimate total daily energy expenditure.

## What Inputs May Be Needed?

Depending on the formula, the calculator may ask for age, height, weight, and sex, along with an activity level. These inputs produce an estimate, not a direct measurement of metabolism.

## How to Use It

1. Enter the requested body measurements and age accurately.
2. Select the activity level that best reflects a typical week.
3. Calculate the estimated BMR and daily calorie needs.
4. Treat the number as a starting point and compare it with real-world trends over time.

## Why Results Vary

Energy needs vary with body composition, health, sleep, medications, and activity. Two people with similar measurements may have different needs. Fitness trackers and online formulas also have limitations.

## Use Estimates Responsibly

Avoid treating a calculator result as a strict prescription. Extreme calorie restriction can be harmful, and calorie needs may differ during pregnancy, adolescence, illness, or recovery. A registered dietitian or qualified healthcare professional can help tailor nutrition to individual circumstances.

## Important Note

This tool is for general education and planning, not diagnosis or treatment. If you have a medical condition or concerns about nutrition, seek personalized professional advice.
