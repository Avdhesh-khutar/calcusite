# Marks Percentage Calculator: Formula for Exam Results and Grades

A **Marks Percentage Calculator** converts marks into a percentage so students can compare performance across tests with different maximum scores.

## Percentage Formula

**Percentage = (Marks Obtained ÷ Total Marks) × 100**

For example, 432 marks out of 500 gives 432 ÷ 500 × 100 = **86.4%**.

## How to Use the Calculator

1. Add the marks you obtained.
2. Enter the maximum possible marks for the same set of assessments.
3. Calculate the percentage.
4. Check whether your school or board excludes any subject or uses a special grading rule.

## When Subjects Have Different Maximum Marks

If subjects have different maximum marks, add the marks obtained and divide by the sum of the corresponding maximum marks for a combined percentage—provided the institution uses a total-mark method. Do not average subject percentages equally unless that is the official rule.

## Percentage vs CGPA

Percentage and CGPA are not universally interchangeable. Institutions and education boards may use different conversion rules. Use the conversion method officially prescribed by your school, college, or examination board rather than assuming one formula applies everywhere.

## Check Your Result

Verify the maximum marks, practical components, internal assessments, and any rounding rules. A calculator can perform the arithmetic accurately only when the entered values and grading method are correct.
