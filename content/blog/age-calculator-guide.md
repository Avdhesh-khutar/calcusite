# Age Calculator: Calculate Your Exact Age in Years, Months, and Days

An **Age Calculator** calculates the time between a date of birth and a selected date. It can show age in years, months, and days, or calculate the duration between two dates for forms, planning, and personal records.

## How Age Is Calculated

Age is based on completed birthdays, not simply the difference between calendar years. If someone was born on 20 December 2000, they have not reached their next age on 19 December 2026; they turn 26 on 20 December 2026.

A date-based calculator compares the year, month, and day and accounts for whether the birthday has occurred in the selected year.

## Example

Suppose a person was born on 15 March 2000 and the selected date is 17 October 2026. Their completed age is **26 years**, with additional months and days since their 26th birthday. Exact day counts depend on the calendar dates used.

## How to Use an Age Calculator

1. Enter the date of birth.
2. Select the date to calculate age on, if the tool supports a custom date.
3. Calculate the result.
4. Check the date format to avoid swapping day and month.

## Leap Years and Birthdays

A leap year contains 29 February. For someone born on that date, how a birthday is treated in a non-leap year can depend on the legal or administrative rule relevant to the situation. If you need age for an official purpose, follow the applicable authority's rules.

Date and time zones can also matter when a birth record includes a time and the dates are interpreted in different locations. Most everyday age calculators use calendar dates rather than exact times.

## Age vs. Date Difference

Completed age answers how many birthdays have passed. A date-difference calculator instead counts the duration between two dates. Depending on whether the start date is included, the total day count may differ by one day.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I calculate age on a past or future date?

If the calculator allows a custom reference date, yes. This can help with planning or checking age on a particular date.

### Why do different tools sometimes show different day counts?

They may use different date formats, inclusivity rules, or time-zone assumptions. Confirm the input dates and the tool's counting convention.

### Is an online age calculation accepted for official forms?

Use the date of birth on your official records and follow the instructions of the institution requesting the information.

## Final Thoughts

An Age Calculator is a convenient way to estimate completed age or the time between dates. For legal, educational, or government paperwork, always follow the relevant authority's rules and verify the dates against official records.
