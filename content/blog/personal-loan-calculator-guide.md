Personal Loan Calculator: Complete Guide

A personal loan can help you manage planned expenses, medical bills, education costs, or unexpected financial needs. Before borrowing, it is important to understand your monthly EMI, total interest, and overall repayment amount.

A Personal Loan Calculator helps you estimate these costs before applying for a loan.

What Is a Personal Loan Calculator?

A personal loan calculator is an online tool that estimates your monthly Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI) based on three main inputs:

- Loan amount
- Annual interest rate
- Loan tenure

It can help you compare repayment options and plan your monthly budget.

Personal Loan EMI Formula

The standard EMI formula is:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ [(1 + r)ⁿ − 1]

Where:

- P = Principal loan amount
- r = Monthly interest rate expressed as a decimal
- n = Total number of monthly instalments

To convert an annual interest rate into a monthly rate, divide it by 12 and then by 100.

For example, an annual interest rate of 12% gives a monthly rate of 0.01.

Personal Loan EMI Example

Suppose you borrow ₹2,00,000 at an annual interest rate of 12% for 3 years.

- Loan amount: ₹2,00,000
- Annual interest rate: 12%
- Loan tenure: 36 months
- Estimated monthly EMI: ₹6,643
- Estimated total repayment: ₹2,39,148
- Estimated total interest: ₹39,148

These figures are approximate and may vary slightly due to rounding. Actual lender calculations, fees, and loan terms may differ.

How to Use a Personal Loan Calculator

Follow these simple steps:

1. Enter the amount you want to borrow.
2. Enter the annual interest rate offered by the lender.
3. Select the repayment tenure in months or years.
4. Calculate your estimated monthly EMI.
5. Review the total repayment and interest amount.
6. Adjust the loan amount or tenure to compare different options.

How Loan Tenure Affects Your EMI

A longer tenure generally reduces the monthly EMI but may increase the total interest paid over the life of the loan.

A shorter tenure generally increases the monthly EMI but can reduce the total interest cost.

Choose a tenure that balances affordable monthly payments with a reasonable overall borrowing cost.

Tips Before Applying for a Personal Loan

- Compare interest rates from multiple lenders.
- Check processing fees and other applicable charges.
- Review the repayment schedule and loan agreement.
- Choose an EMI that fits comfortably within your monthly budget.
- Check the lender's eligibility requirements and applicable late-payment charges.
- Avoid borrowing more than you need.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a personal loan calculator free?

Many online personal loan calculators are free to use. You can estimate EMI and repayment costs without submitting a loan application.

Does a personal loan calculator guarantee loan approval?

No. A calculator only provides estimates. Loan approval depends on the lender's eligibility criteria, income assessment, credit history, and other factors.

Does a lower interest rate always mean a cheaper loan?

A lower interest rate can reduce borrowing costs, but you should also compare processing fees, tenure, and other applicable charges.

Can I repay a personal loan early?

Some lenders allow early repayment or foreclosure subject to the loan agreement and applicable charges. Check the terms with your lender before making a decision.
