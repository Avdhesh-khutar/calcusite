# PPF Calculator: Estimate Public Provident Fund Growth

A **Public Provident Fund (PPF) Calculator** helps estimate how regular contributions may accumulate over the account's long tenure. PPF is a government-backed savings scheme in India, but rates, contribution limits, and rules should always be checked against current official information.

## How a PPF Estimate Works

A calculator generally uses the annual contribution, assumed interest rate, and investment period to estimate the balance. The official PPF interest rate can change, so an estimate that assumes one rate throughout the term may differ from the actual account balance.

## How to Use a PPF Calculator

1. Enter the amount you plan to contribute.
2. Choose whether the contribution is monthly or annual if supported.
3. Enter the assumed annual interest rate.
4. Set the period and calculate the estimated balance and interest.

## Understand the Rules

Before opening or contributing to a PPF account, confirm the current minimum and maximum annual contributions, deposit timing rules, maturity period, extension options, withdrawal conditions, and tax treatment. These details can change or depend on account circumstances.

## Contribution Timing Matters

For savings products where interest is calculated using account balances on specified dates, the timing of deposits may affect interest. Use the provider's official guidance rather than assuming every deposit earns a full year's interest.

## PPF and Other Savings Options

PPF is generally intended for long-term savings. Compare liquidity, tenure, risk, tax treatment, and access to funds with alternatives such as fixed deposits or market-linked investments.

## Disclaimer

This calculator is for illustration only. It does not replace official PPF rules or an account statement. Verify current terms through official sources before making financial decisions.
