# TDS Calculator: Estimate Tax Deducted at Source

A **TDS Calculator** estimates tax deducted at source from a payment using an entered amount and rate. In India, the applicable TDS section, rate, threshold, and conditions depend on the nature of the payment and the taxpayer's circumstances.

## Basic Calculation

**Estimated TDS = Payment Amount × TDS Rate ÷ 100**

**Estimated Net Payment = Payment Amount − TDS**

For example, if a hypothetical payment is ₹20,000 and an illustrative deduction rate is 5%, estimated TDS is ₹1,000 and the net payment is ₹19,000. This example does not establish the legally applicable rate.

## How to Use a TDS Calculator

1. Enter the payment or amount subject to deduction.
2. Enter the applicable rate after checking the relevant rule.
3. Calculate the estimated deduction and net amount.
4. Confirm thresholds, exemptions, and any other rules that apply to the payment category.

## Why the Correct Section Matters

TDS rules can differ for salary, professional fees, rent, interest, commissions, and other payments. A rate entered without checking the correct provision can produce a misleading result.

## Keep Records

Compare deductions with the payer's documentation and tax records. If a deduction seems incorrect, seek clarification from the payer or a qualified tax professional.

This calculator is for general estimation only, not tax or legal advice. Verify current rules and official guidance before making a deduction or filing a return.
