# Water Intake Calculator: Estimate Daily Hydration Needs

A **Water Intake Calculator** offers a rough starting estimate for daily fluid intake. Hydration needs vary widely, so a single number cannot suit everyone.

## What Affects Water Needs?

Fluid needs depend on body size, activity, temperature, humidity, diet, illness, pregnancy or breastfeeding, and individual health. People who exercise or work outdoors in hot weather may need more fluids than someone resting in a cool environment.

## How to Use an Estimate

1. Enter the requested details, such as body weight or activity level.
2. Review the estimated daily amount.
3. Spread fluids across the day and adjust to your circumstances.
4. Remember that drinks and water-rich foods can also contribute to fluid intake.

## Signs and Context

Thirst is a useful signal for many healthy adults, and urine colour can provide a rough clue, though neither is perfect. Needs can change during heat, exercise, fever, vomiting, or diarrhoea.

## Important Safety Note

Do not force excessive amounts of water; drinking too much too quickly can be dangerous. People with kidney, heart, liver, or other conditions may have specific fluid restrictions and should follow their clinician's advice.

## Bottom Line

Treat calculator results as general educational estimates, not a diagnosis or strict prescription. If you have ongoing symptoms of dehydration or concerns about fluid balance, seek qualified medical advice.
