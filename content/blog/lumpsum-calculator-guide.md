# Lumpsum Calculator: Estimate Future Value of a One-Time Investment

A **Lumpsum Calculator** estimates the possible future value of money invested once. It is often used to compare scenarios for a one-time investment over different periods and assumed annual returns.

## How the Calculation Works

For an assumed annual compounded return:

**Future Value = Investment × (1 + Return ÷ 100)^Years**

If ₹50,000 grows at an assumed 8% annually for 5 years, the estimate is ₹50,000 × 1.08⁵, or about ₹73,466. The estimated gain is about ₹23,466 before taxes, fees, and inflation.

## How to Use a Lumpsum Calculator

1. Enter the one-time investment amount.
2. Enter an assumed annual return.
3. Select the investment duration.
4. Calculate the future value, invested amount, and estimated gain.
5. Change one input at a time to compare scenarios.

## Lumpsum vs SIP

A lumpsum invests money at once. A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) invests smaller amounts at regular intervals. A lumpsum can be exposed to market movements immediately, while an SIP spreads contributions over time. Neither approach guarantees a profit.

## Choose Assumptions Carefully

The return field is an assumption, not a promise. Market-linked investments fluctuate, and actual results depend on asset allocation, costs, taxes, and market performance. Inflation also reduces what a future amount can buy.

## Useful Planning Tips

- Keep emergency savings separate from long-term investments.
- Match the investment risk to your time horizon and ability to handle losses.
- Compare conservative, moderate, and optimistic scenarios.
- Review fees and tax rules before acting.

Use the estimate as a planning aid, not as a guaranteed forecast or personalized investment advice.
