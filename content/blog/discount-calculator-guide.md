# Discount Calculator: How to Calculate Sale Prices and Savings

A **Discount Calculator** helps you find how much you save during a sale and what you will actually pay after a discount. It is useful when comparing offers on clothing, electronics, household goods, and online shopping.

## Discount Formula

**Discount Amount = Original Price × (Discount Percentage ÷ 100)**

**Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount**

### Example: 25% off

Suppose a jacket costs ₹2,400 and the store offers a 25% discount.

- Original price: ₹2,400
- Discount: 25%
- Savings: ₹2,400 × 0.25 = ₹600
- Final price: ₹2,400 − ₹600 = **₹1,800**

This calculation assumes the discount applies to the full original price before any additional taxes, delivery fees, or other charges.

## How to Use a Discount Calculator

1. Enter the original listed price.
2. Enter the discount percentage.
3. Calculate the discount amount and sale price.
4. Add any applicable taxes, delivery charges, or fees to estimate the final checkout total.

## How to Compare Two Offers

A larger discount percentage does not always mean the best deal. Compare the final prices for the same product and check whether shipping, warranty, returns, or bundle conditions differ.

For example, a 20% discount on ₹3,000 saves ₹600, while a 30% discount on ₹1,800 saves ₹540. The second offer has a higher percentage discount but a smaller rupee saving.

## Successive Discounts

If a store applies 20% off and then another 10% off the reduced price, the discounts are usually applied one after another—not added to make 30%.

For a ₹1,000 item:
- After 20% off: ₹800
- After another 10% off ₹800: ₹720
- Total saving: ₹280, or 28% of the original price

Always read the offer terms to confirm how stacked discounts work.

## Common Discount Calculator Mistakes

- Applying a discount to the wrong price.
- Assuming two successive discounts can simply be added.
- Forgetting delivery fees or taxes that may apply at checkout.
- Comparing different product sizes, versions, or warranty terms.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I find the price after 10% off?

Multiply the original price by 0.90. For a ₹500 item, the price after 10% off is ₹450.

### How do I find the discount percentage from two prices?

Divide the saving by the original price and multiply by 100.

### Is the displayed sale price always the final price?

Not necessarily. Taxes, shipping, and other charges may be added at checkout depending on the seller and local rules.

## Final Thoughts

A Discount Calculator makes offers easier to compare by showing both the amount saved and the price payable. Use the final checkout total—not only the advertised discount—to decide which offer is best.

*Examples are illustrative. Actual prices and offer conditions depend on the seller.*
