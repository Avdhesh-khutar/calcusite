# Attendance Calculator: Find Your Attendance Percentage and Required Classes

An **Attendance Calculator** helps students calculate the percentage of classes attended and compare it with a target set by their institution.

## Attendance Formula

**Attendance Percentage = Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes × 100**

For example, if you attended 36 of 45 classes, your attendance is 36 ÷ 45 × 100 = **80%**.

## How to Use the Calculator

1. Enter the number of classes attended.
2. Enter the total number of classes held.
3. Calculate the attendance percentage.
4. If a target feature is available, enter the required percentage to estimate future attendance needs.

## How Many Classes Must You Attend?

To estimate the number of consecutive classes needed to reach a target, the calculation must account for both attended classes and the growing total. For target rate (T), attended classes (A), and total classes (N), the minimum additional classes (x) satisfy:

**(A + x) ÷ (N + x) ≥ T ÷ 100**

The result is only an estimate; institutional rules may count practicals, approved absences, or different course components separately.

## Avoid Common Mistakes

- Do not divide attended classes by classes missed.
- Make sure the total includes the same course and period as the attended count.
- Check whether your institution rounds percentages.
- Confirm the official attendance policy and any exceptions.

Use the calculator for planning, and verify your official attendance record with your school or college.
