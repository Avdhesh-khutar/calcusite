# Date Difference Calculator: Count Days Between Two Dates

A **Date Difference Calculator** finds the time between two calendar dates. It is useful for planning travel, deadlines, projects, leave, and personal milestones.

## How Date Difference Works

Enter a start date and an end date. The calculator compares the dates and reports the elapsed duration, often in days and sometimes in weeks, months, or years.

For example, the elapsed time from 1 October to 8 October is 7 days when the start date is not counted as a full elapsed day. If both dates are counted inclusively, the count is 8 calendar dates. Always check which convention a task requires.

## How to Use It

1. Select the start date.
2. Select the end date.
3. Calculate the difference.
4. Confirm whether you need elapsed days or inclusive calendar-day counting.

## Leap Years and Month Lengths

Calendar months have different lengths, and leap years add 29 February. Accurate date calculations should use calendar dates rather than multiplying months by a fixed number of days.

## Business Days vs Calendar Days

A standard date difference usually counts calendar days, including weekends. Business-day calculations may exclude weekends and public holidays, which vary by country and organization.

## Common Uses

- Counting days until a deadline or event.
- Estimating the length of a trip or project.
- Checking elapsed time between two dates.
- Planning leave or tracking milestones.

For legal, contractual, or official deadlines, confirm the applicable counting rules and local holidays. A date calculator is a convenience tool, not a substitute for official guidance.
