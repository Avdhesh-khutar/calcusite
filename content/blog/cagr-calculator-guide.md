# CAGR Calculator: Annualized Growth Rate Formula and Example

**Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR)** describes the smoothed annual growth rate that would take a starting value to an ending value over a chosen period, assuming compounding.

## CAGR Formula

**CAGR = [(Ending Value ÷ Starting Value)^(1 ÷ Years) − 1] × 100**

For example, if an investment grows from ₹10,000 to ₹15,000 over 4 years, CAGR is approximately 10.67% per year. This is an annualized rate, not a statement that the investment gained exactly 10.67% in each individual year.

## How to Use a CAGR Calculator

1. Enter the starting value.
2. Enter the ending value.
3. Enter the number of years between them.
4. Calculate the annualized rate and review the result.

Starting value and time must be greater than zero. If the ending value is below the starting value, CAGR can be negative, representing a decline over the period.

## Why CAGR Is Useful

CAGR helps compare growth over different periods and makes uneven growth easier to summarize. It is commonly used for business revenue, sales, fund values, and other measures that change over time.

## What CAGR Cannot Tell You

CAGR hides the path taken between the two values. Two investments can have the same CAGR but very different volatility and risk. It also does not account automatically for additional contributions, withdrawals, taxes, fees, or inflation.

## Practical Tip

Compare CAGR with the full context: the period measured, risk, cash flows, and any costs. Past growth does not guarantee future performance, especially for market-linked investments.
