# Add or Subtract Days Calculator: Find a Future or Past Date

An **Add or Subtract Days Calculator** finds a future or past date by moving a selected number of calendar days from a starting date.

## How It Works

Choose a starting date, enter the number of days, and choose whether to add or subtract them. The result should account for different month lengths, year boundaries, and leap years.

## How to Use It

1. Select the starting date.
2. Enter the number of days.
3. Choose Add or Subtract.
4. Calculate and verify the resulting date.

## Calendar Days vs Business Days

Adding 10 calendar days includes weekends and holidays. Adding 10 business days usually excludes weekends and may exclude public holidays, depending on the rule being used. A basic date calculator may not account for local holidays.

## Inclusive Counting

Some official processes count the starting day as day one; others count elapsed days after the starting date. This difference can change a deadline by one day. Confirm the applicable rule for legal, financial, or administrative deadlines.

## Useful Applications

This tool can help with trip planning, project timelines, reminders, and personal milestones. For official deadlines, confirm the final date with the relevant organization.
