# CGPA Calculator Guide: Calculate Grade Point Average Correctly

**Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA)** summarizes academic performance using grade points. The exact method depends on the institution's grading system and whether subjects carry different credits.

## Simple CGPA and Credit-Weighted CGPA

If every subject has equal weight, a simple average of grade points may be appropriate. If subjects carry different credits, many systems use a weighted formula:

**CGPA = Sum of (Grade Point × Subject Credits) ÷ Sum of Subject Credits**

For example, grade points should not be averaged equally when a 4-credit subject counts more than a 1-credit subject under the institution's rules.

## How to Calculate It

1. Record the grade point for each subject.
2. Record the credit value for each subject if applicable.
3. Multiply each grade point by its credits.
4. Add the weighted points and divide by total credits.
5. Check rounding and any excluded or repeated subjects under the official policy.

## CGPA to Percentage

There is no single conversion factor that applies to every school, university, or board. Some institutions publish a specific formula; others use a grade-to-percentage table. Follow the official academic regulations or transcript guidance.

## Common Mistakes

- Averaging grades without accounting for credits.
- Using the wrong grade-point scale.
- Applying an unofficial percentage multiplier.
- Including subjects that the institution excludes from the calculation.

A calculator can help with arithmetic, but your institution's grading policy determines the official CGPA and percentage.
