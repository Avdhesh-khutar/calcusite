# RD Calculator: Estimate Recurring Deposit Maturity and Interest

A **Recurring Deposit (RD) Calculator** estimates how much your regular monthly deposits may grow by the end of a chosen term. It is useful when you want to save a fixed amount every month rather than invest a lump sum at once.

## What Is a Recurring Deposit?

An RD lets you deposit a set amount at regular intervals, usually monthly, for a fixed tenure. The bank or post office applies its stated interest rules. Actual maturity depends on the institution's compounding method, deposit dates, rate, and applicable tax.

## How to Estimate RD Maturity

Enter your monthly deposit, annual interest rate, and tenure. The calculator estimates the total maturity value and separates the money deposited from the interest earned.

For example, depositing ₹2,000 per month for 2 years means total deposits of ₹48,000 before interest. The final amount will be higher if interest is earned; the exact figure depends on the provider's terms.

## How to Use an RD Calculator

1. Enter the monthly deposit you can comfortably afford.
2. Enter the annual interest rate offered by your bank or provider.
3. Select the tenure in years or months, as supported.
4. Calculate and compare different monthly amounts and terms.

## What to Check Before Opening an RD

- Confirm the current rate and whether it is fixed for the full term.
- Check premature-closure rules, penalties, and missed-installment rules.
- Consider tax treatment of interest and your overall savings goals.
- Keep an emergency fund accessible instead of locking away all savings.

## RD vs FD

An RD builds savings through repeated deposits. A Fixed Deposit (FD) generally invests one lump sum at the start. The better fit depends on whether you already have money available or prefer saving gradually.

## Important Note

Calculator outputs are estimates, not bank quotes. Always verify the institution's maturity calculation, terms, and tax treatment before investing.
