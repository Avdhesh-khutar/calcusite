# Percentage Calculator: Formula, Examples, and Everyday Uses

Percentages help explain marks, discounts, price changes, budgets, and many other everyday calculations. A **Percentage Calculator** makes it easier to find a percentage of a number, work out what percentage one value is of another, or compare a change between two values.

## What Does Percent Mean?

Percent means “per hundred.” For example, 25% means 25 out of every 100. Percentages make it easier to compare values even when the totals are different.

## Useful Percentage Formulas

### Find a percentage of a number

**Result = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number**

Example: What is 20% of ₹1,500?

20 ÷ 100 × 1,500 = **₹300**.

### Find what percentage one number is of another

**Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100**

Example: You scored 72 marks out of 90.

72 ÷ 90 × 100 = **80%**.

### Calculate percentage increase

**Percentage Increase = ((New Value − Old Value) ÷ Old Value) × 100**

If a price rises from ₹800 to ₹920, the increase is ₹120. The percentage increase is 120 ÷ 800 × 100 = **15%**.

### Calculate percentage decrease

**Percentage Decrease = ((Old Value − New Value) ÷ Old Value) × 100**

If a product drops from ₹2,000 to ₹1,700, the decrease is ₹300, or **15%**.

## How to Use a Percentage Calculator

1. Choose the calculation you need: percentage of a number, part-to-whole percentage, or percentage change.
2. Enter the relevant values.
3. Calculate the result.
4. Check that you selected the correct starting value when measuring a change.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Using the wrong base value:** Percentage change is calculated relative to the original value.
- **Confusing percentage points with percent change:** A rate moving from 10% to 15% rises by 5 percentage points, which is a 50% relative increase.
- **Rounding too early:** Keep full precision during calculations and round the final answer.
- **Dividing by zero:** A percentage relative to a zero original value is undefined using the standard percentage-change formula.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I calculate 10% quickly?

Move the decimal point one place left, or divide the amount by 10. For example, 10% of ₹450 is ₹45.

### How do I calculate marks percentage?

Divide marks obtained by total possible marks and multiply by 100. Make sure both values use the same marking scale.

### Can a percentage be greater than 100%?

Yes. If a value is larger than the reference total, the resulting percentage can exceed 100%.

## Final Thoughts

A Percentage Calculator is useful for school results, shopping discounts, business figures, and tracking changes. Choose the formula that matches your question and always verify which value is the base.
