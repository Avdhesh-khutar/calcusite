# Area Converter: Convert Square Metres, Square Feet, Acres and More

An **Area Converter** changes a measurement from one area unit to another. It is useful for property, construction, gardening, farming, and school mathematics.

## Common Area Conversions

- 1 square metre ≈ 10.7639 square feet
- 1 acre = 4,046.8564 square metres
- 1 hectare = 10,000 square metres
- 1 square kilometre = 1,000,000 square metres

Some local land units vary by region, so confirm the definition used in your location before relying on a conversion for property transactions.

## How to Use an Area Converter

1. Enter the area value.
2. Select the source unit.
3. Select the destination unit.
4. Convert and check the unit displayed with the answer.

## Area vs Length

Area measures a two-dimensional surface and uses squared units, such as m² or ft². A length conversion alone cannot be applied directly to area without squaring the conversion factor.

## Practical Example

A room measuring 4 metres by 5 metres has an area of 20 m². Converting 20 m² gives approximately 215.28 ft².

## Accuracy Tips

Check whether a listing uses carpet area, built-up area, or another property definition; those terms are not interchangeable. For legal documents, construction plans, or land registration, use the units and certified measurements required by the relevant authority.
