# Profit and Loss Calculator: Cost Price, Selling Price and Margin

A **Profit and Loss Calculator** helps determine whether a sale earned a profit or resulted in a loss by comparing cost price and selling price.

## Core Formulas

**Profit = Selling Price − Cost Price**

**Loss = Cost Price − Selling Price**, when selling price is lower than cost price.

**Profit Percentage = Profit ÷ Cost Price × 100**

For example, if an item costs ₹800 and sells for ₹1,000, profit is ₹200 and profit percentage is 25% of cost price.

## How to Use It

1. Enter the cost price.
2. Enter the selling price.
3. Calculate the difference and percentage.
4. Include relevant expenses if you want a more realistic business result.

## Profit Percentage vs Profit Margin

Profit percentage often refers to profit divided by cost price. Profit margin usually means profit divided by selling revenue. With cost ₹800 and selling price ₹1,000, the profit is ₹200: markup on cost is 25%, while margin on sales is 20%.

## Remember Additional Costs

Shipping, packaging, platform fees, payment charges, returns, rent, and taxes can change the true profit. If those costs are omitted, the result is only a simple price comparison.

## Practical Use

Retailers, resellers, and small businesses can use this calculator to compare prices and estimate a basic margin. Keep accurate records and confirm accounting and tax treatment with a qualified professional when needed.
