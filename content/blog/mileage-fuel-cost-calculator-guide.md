# Mileage and Fuel Cost Calculator: Estimate Trip Fuel Expenses

A **Mileage and Fuel Cost Calculator** estimates how much fuel a journey may use and its approximate cost.

## Main Formulas

When mileage is expressed in kilometres per litre:

**Fuel Needed = Distance ÷ Mileage**

**Fuel Cost = Fuel Needed × Price per Litre**

For example, a 300 km trip in a vehicle averaging 15 km/L requires about 20 litres. At ₹100 per litre, the estimated fuel cost is ₹2,000.

## How to Use the Calculator

1. Enter the distance of the trip.
2. Enter expected mileage in km/L.
3. Enter the current fuel price per litre.
4. Calculate fuel required and estimated cost.

## What Affects Mileage?

Actual mileage depends on traffic, speed, road surface, tyre pressure, load, maintenance, air conditioning, driving style, and weather. Manufacturer figures may differ from everyday use.

## Estimate a Round Trip

For a return journey, include the total return distance rather than only the one-way distance. If you plan several stops or detours, add those kilometres too.

## Make the Estimate More Useful

Use a realistic mileage based on your own recent driving, check the local pump price, and leave a buffer for traffic or route changes. The calculation excludes tolls, parking, maintenance, and other travel expenses unless entered separately.

This is a planning estimate, not a guarantee of actual fuel consumption.
