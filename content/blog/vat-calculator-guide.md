# VAT Calculator: Add or Remove Value Added Tax

A **VAT Calculator** estimates value added tax on a price or extracts the tax component from a VAT-inclusive total. VAT rules and rates differ by country and product category.

## Add VAT to a Price

**VAT = Net Price × VAT Rate ÷ 100**

**Gross Price = Net Price + VAT**

For example, a net price of ₹1,000 with an illustrative 10% VAT gives ₹100 tax and a gross price of ₹1,100. This example is mathematical only; it does not imply that a 10% VAT rate applies in a particular location.

## Remove VAT from an Inclusive Price

**Net Price = Gross Price ÷ (1 + VAT Rate ÷ 100)**

**VAT Amount = Gross Price − Net Price**

Do not calculate the tax-inclusive component simply by multiplying the gross total by the rate; the rate applies to the net price.

## How to Use the Calculator

1. Enter the price.
2. Enter the applicable VAT rate.
3. Choose whether to add or remove VAT.
4. Calculate the net price, tax amount, and total.

## Important Considerations

Check local rules for exemptions, reduced rates, rounding, invoices, and registration obligations. VAT is not identical to GST in every jurisdiction, and tax obligations depend on applicable law.

Use this calculator for estimates only. For filing or compliance, consult official tax guidance or a qualified tax professional.
