# Marks Needed Calculator: Find the Score Required for Your Target

A **Marks Needed Calculator** helps students estimate the score required in a remaining test or exam to reach a target total or percentage.

## Basic Formula

If all marks contribute equally and the target is a percentage of the combined total:

**Target Marks = Target Percentage × Total Possible Marks ÷ 100**

If you already have marks from completed assessments, subtract those marks from the target total to estimate what remains. The exact method depends on the grading structure.

## Example

Suppose a course has 500 total marks and your target is 80%. You need 400 marks overall. If you have already earned 250 marks, you need another 150 marks from the remaining assessments, provided the course uses a straightforward total-mark system.

## How to Use the Calculator

1. Enter the target percentage or total score.
2. Enter the maximum possible marks.
3. Add marks already earned if the tool asks for them.
4. Calculate the required marks and check that they do not exceed the marks still available.

## Weighted Assessments

Some courses assign different weights to assignments, practicals, and exams. In that case, calculate using weighted scores rather than simply adding raw marks. Check the official syllabus for rounding and minimum subject requirements.

## Plan Realistically

Use the result to plan revision time and identify areas that need attention. The calculator estimates a target; it cannot predict exam difficulty or guarantee a score.
