# Tip and Bill Split Calculator: Share a Restaurant Bill Fairly

A **Tip and Bill Split Calculator** helps estimate a gratuity, the final amount, and how much each person pays when a bill is shared.

## Basic Formulas

**Tip = Bill Amount × Tip Percentage ÷ 100**

**Total = Bill Amount + Tip**

**Per-Person Share = Total ÷ Number of People**

For example, on a ₹2,000 bill with a 10% tip, the tip is ₹200 and the total is ₹2,200. Split equally between four people, each share is ₹550.

## How to Use It

1. Enter the bill before tip.
2. Enter the tip percentage if you want to include one.
3. Enter the number of people sharing the bill.
4. Calculate the tip, total, and per-person amount.

## Check the Bill First

Some restaurants already include a service charge, and local customs about tipping vary. Review the bill so you do not unintentionally add a tip on top of a charge you did not intend to pay.

## Unequal Shares

A basic bill-split calculator divides the total equally. If some people ordered different items, you may need to allocate items and taxes individually before splitting the remainder.

## Rounding

When the total does not divide evenly, one or more people may need to pay a small rounding difference. Agree on the method before settling up.

Use the calculator as a convenience tool; confirm the bill and any applicable charges directly with the service provider.
