# Average Calculator: Mean Formula, Examples and Common Uses

An **Average Calculator** commonly finds the arithmetic mean of a list of numbers.

## Average Formula

**Average = Sum of All Values ÷ Number of Values**

For example, the average of 10, 15, and 20 is (10 + 15 + 20) ÷ 3 = 15.

## How to Use It

1. Enter the values you want to compare.
2. Make sure the values use the same unit and describe comparable things.
3. Calculate the average.
4. Review unusually high or low values that may influence the result.

## Mean vs Other Averages

The arithmetic mean is not the only measure of a typical value. The median is the middle value after sorting; the mode is the most frequent value. For skewed data, the median may better represent a typical observation.

A weighted average gives some values more influence than others. For example, course grades with different assignment weights should not always be averaged as if every assignment counts equally.

## Common Uses

Averages can summarize marks, expenses, measurements, scores, or repeated observations. But the average alone does not show the spread or range of values.

Check for missing values, duplicate entries, and mixed units. A calculator performs the arithmetic on the values provided; it cannot determine whether the data itself is appropriate.
