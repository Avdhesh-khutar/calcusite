# Break-Even Calculator: Find the Sales Needed to Cover Costs

A **Break-Even Calculator** helps a business estimate the sales volume at which total revenue equals total costs. At this point, the business has neither a profit nor a loss before other factors not included in the model.

## Break-Even Formula

**Break-Even Units = Fixed Costs ÷ (Selling Price per Unit − Variable Cost per Unit)**

The difference between selling price and variable cost is called contribution per unit. The formula is useful only when contribution is positive.

## Example

Suppose monthly fixed costs are ₹60,000, a product sells for ₹500, and variable cost is ₹300 per unit. Contribution is ₹200 per unit, so break-even volume is ₹60,000 ÷ ₹200 = **300 units**. Selling more than 300 units may create an operating profit under these assumptions; selling fewer may leave a shortfall.

## How to Use a Break-Even Calculator

1. Enter fixed costs for the period.
2. Enter selling price per unit.
3. Enter variable cost per unit.
4. Calculate the break-even units or sales revenue.
5. Compare the result with realistic demand and production capacity.

## Include the Right Costs

Fixed costs may include rent and salaries that do not change directly with each unit. Variable costs may include materials, packaging, and per-sale fees. Some costs are mixed, so estimates should reflect how they behave at different volumes.

## Limitations

The basic model assumes prices and costs stay stable and that units sold can be estimated. Discounts, returns, taxes, capacity limits, and multiple products can change the result.

Use break-even analysis as a planning tool, and review assumptions regularly as your business changes.
