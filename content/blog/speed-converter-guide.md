# Speed Converter: Convert km/h, mph and Metres per Second

A **Speed Converter** changes speed measurements between units used in travel, science, sport, and engineering.

## Useful Conversion Factors

- 1 km/h ≈ 0.621371 mph
- 1 mph ≈ 1.60934 km/h
- 1 m/s = 3.6 km/h
- 1 knot = 1.852 km/h

## How to Use It

1. Enter the speed value.
2. Choose the unit you are converting from.
3. Choose the unit you want.
4. Review the converted number and unit.

## Example

A speed of 90 km/h is approximately 55.92 mph. To convert 20 m/s to km/h, multiply by 3.6 to get 72 km/h.

## Keep Context in Mind

Speed differs from velocity, which includes direction. For everyday vehicle use, follow local speed-limit signs and the units displayed by your vehicle or navigation system.

## Rounding

Conversion results may be rounded for display. For technical work, retain enough decimal places for the required precision and use the same unit system throughout a calculation.
